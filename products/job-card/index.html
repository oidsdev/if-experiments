Write the job down before you hand it to an agent.

An agent job goes sideways when the first message is fuzzy: no picture of done, no named files, nothing that says stop.

Job Card is a form for one job. You fill in what done looks like, what the agent may touch, what it must leave alone, and when it should stop. The page writes a short brief you can copy, download, or print.

Your answers stay in this browser. This page does not send them anywhere.

What you get

  • A single-page form
  • A plain-text brief, with blank sections left out
  • Copy, download, and print

What it does not do

It does not run the agent or check the work. You still read the changes. The brief is a clearer first message, not a guarantee.
