    CA B&P 17941
    
    
    California Business and Professions Code section 17941 is about bots that hide what they are. It applies when a person uses a bot online to mislead someone in California, on purpose, to push a sale or influence a vote. The section says a person is not liable under it if they disclose that it is a bot. The disclosure has to be clear, conspicuous, and reasonably designed to inform the person.
