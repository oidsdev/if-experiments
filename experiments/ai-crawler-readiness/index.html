Orbital Desk

AI crawler readiness

Paste robots.txt, llms.txt, and a page you want cited. The check runs in this browser. It does not fetch your site.

What it checks

  • llms.txt has a title, a short summary, and links.
  • GPTBot, ClaudeBot, and PerplexityBot may fetch the homepage.
  • The page declares an FAQ in JSON-LD.

Ready means all three passed. Needs work means one failed, or a check is only partly met. Not ready means two or more failed.

Fixes

    Runs in this browser. No upload, no account, no tracking.
